2. What the App does

The App generates and organizes number combinations, saves picks and wheels, displays public main-draw, Maxplus and Maxmillions results when available, and main-draw statistics, and provides simulations and number filters. It does not sell lottery tickets, place wagers, accept deposits, transfer money, or award cash prizes. Saved combinations are not lottery tickets or proof of purchase. Strategies, statistics and simulations do not predict results or guarantee winnings.

The App is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lotto Max, the Interprovincial Lottery Corporation, Loto-Québec, OLG or any other Canadian lottery operator. Lottery names and marks belong to their respective owners.

6. Results and external links

The App retrieves published Lotto Max results over HTTPS from Loto-Québec’s public results pages, including dated results pages. These are web pages, not a developer-operated results server. Requests may include the selected drawing date, but do not include your saved picks; matching is performed locally in the App. The results host receives normal connection information such as your IP address and request metadata. See Loto-Québec’s privacy information. Visiting its website separately may involve additional practices described by that service.

Links to official lottery information, privacy pages or advertisements open third-party services. Those services handle any information you submit to them under their own policies. This App does not control their content or practices.

7. Retention, deletion and security

Local App information remains until you delete it through available App controls, clear App storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App. To clear storage, use Android Settings → Apps → Lotto Max Generator → Storage → Clear data/storage; labels vary by device. This removes local picks, cached results and reward credits and may reset preferences. The App disables automatic Android backup, but device manufacturers’ transfer or restore behavior can vary.

The developer does not maintain a remote copy of your local picks or credits and cannot restore them after deletion. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. You may request its deletion, subject to applicable exceptions. Google, the results service and other providers apply their own retention practices; clearing local App storage does not delete their records.

The App uses Android’s private application storage for local data. Results requests use HTTPS. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts user data in transit with TLS. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not email passwords, full card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.
